In Manglik analysis, Mars is checked in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th houses from the Lagna, the Moon and Venus. Traditionally this placement indicates strong energy and a need for patience and understanding in partnership. It is weighed together with cancellation factors and the partner's chart. A Manglik placement is found from: the Lagna, the Moon or Venus. No cancellation factor applies, so the traditional view treats it as active. Matching with the partner's chart is customary, and mutual placements are considered to balance each other. Traditional practices include worshipping Hanuman on Tuesdays and reciting the Mangal mantra. They are devotional and cultural; they do not decide the quality of a relationship.
