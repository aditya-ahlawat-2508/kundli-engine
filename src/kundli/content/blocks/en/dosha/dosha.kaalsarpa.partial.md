Six of the seven planets lie on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis, a partial Kaal Sarp pattern. Traditionally its effect is considered milder than a full formation. Kaal Sarp Yoga is said to form when the seven planets lie on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis. Traditionally it is read as a theme of karmic intensity: results may arrive in bursts or after delay, and attention gathers on the houses of the nodal axis. It is one indicator among many, and many charts with this pattern show strong achievement.
