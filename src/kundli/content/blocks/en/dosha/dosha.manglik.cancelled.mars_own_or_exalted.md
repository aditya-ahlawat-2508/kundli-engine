In Manglik analysis, Mars is checked in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th houses from the Lagna, the Moon and Venus. Traditionally this placement indicates strong energy and a need for patience and understanding in partnership. It is weighed together with cancellation factors and the partner's chart. A Manglik placement is found from the Lagna, the Moon or Venus, but traditional cancellation factors apply (for example Mars in its own sign or exalted, or Jupiter's association or aspect), which reduce its weight.
