Sade Sati is the roughly seven-and-a-half-year period when Saturn transits the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon. Traditionally it is a period of testing, responsibility and restructuring, and many people describe it as a time of maturity. Because Saturn can move back and forth across a sign boundary, the table below lists every entry and exit. At the reference date of this report Sade Sati is active (Dhaiya (Saturn 4th from the Moon)). Traditionally advised: steady routines, honesty, patience and respect for elders and colleagues. Traditional practices include reciting the Hanuman Chalisa, serving elders and the needy, and charity on Saturdays. They are devotional and cultural; no outcome is guaranteed.
