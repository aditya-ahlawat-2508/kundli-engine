Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, energy is patient and sustained, best used on concrete projects that need stamina. The sign lord Venus is neutral to Mars, giving moderate and mixed support.
