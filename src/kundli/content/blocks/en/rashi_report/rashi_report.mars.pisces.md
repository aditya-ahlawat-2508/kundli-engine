Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, energy is emotional and protective, strong when motivated by feeling and prone to moods. The sign lord Jupiter is friendly to Mars, so the placement is generally supportive.
