Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, discipline is natural and enduring, giving patience, thrift and capacity for hard work. Saturn is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
