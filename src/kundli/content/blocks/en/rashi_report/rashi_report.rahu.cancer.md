Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, ambition mixes with emotion and imagination, drawn to the hidden, the foreign or the mystical. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
