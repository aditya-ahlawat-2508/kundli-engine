Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, ambition focuses on money, status and tangible achievement, often by unconventional means. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
