Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, emotions are quick and warm, reacting with enthusiasm and a need for action. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
