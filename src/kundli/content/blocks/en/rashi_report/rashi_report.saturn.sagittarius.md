Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, discipline is tested by impatience and learns to channel drive into long-term goals. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Saturn, giving moderate and mixed support.
