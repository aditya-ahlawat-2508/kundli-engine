Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, detachment shows as deep intuition and a pull toward inner, spiritual experience. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
