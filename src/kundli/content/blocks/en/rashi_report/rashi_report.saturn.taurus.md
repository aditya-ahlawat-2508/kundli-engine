Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, discipline is natural and enduring, giving patience, thrift and capacity for hard work. The sign lord Venus is friendly to Saturn, so the placement is generally supportive.
