Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, discipline meets emotion, with caution in feelings and a need to build trust slowly. The sign lord Mars is not friendly to Saturn, so results may come with some effort.
