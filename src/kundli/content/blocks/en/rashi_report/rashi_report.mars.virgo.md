Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, energy is patient and sustained, best used on concrete projects that need stamina. The sign lord Mercury is not friendly to Mars, so results may come with some effort.
