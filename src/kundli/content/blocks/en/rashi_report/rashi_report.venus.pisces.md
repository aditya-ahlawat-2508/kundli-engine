Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, affection is tender and devoted, emotionally deep and romantic. Venus is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
