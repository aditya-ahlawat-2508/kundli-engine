Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, confidence is steady and practical, built through reliable effort and visible results. The sign lord Mercury is neutral to Sun, giving moderate and mixed support.
