Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, affection is passionate and expressive, drawn to excitement and admiration. The sign lord Mars is neutral to Venus, giving moderate and mixed support.
