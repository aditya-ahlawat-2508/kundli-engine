Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, discipline works through systems, rules and ideas, with serious, detached reasoning. Saturn is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
