Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, thinking is fast, decisive and persuasive, sometimes impatient with detail. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Mercury, giving moderate and mixed support.
