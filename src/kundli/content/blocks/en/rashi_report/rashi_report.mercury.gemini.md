Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, thinking is versatile and social, quick to learn, connect and exchange ideas. Mercury is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
