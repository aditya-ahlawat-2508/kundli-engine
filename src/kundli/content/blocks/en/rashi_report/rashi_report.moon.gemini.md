Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, emotions are processed through thought and conversation, with a curious, restless mind. The sign lord Mercury is friendly to Moon, so the placement is generally supportive.
