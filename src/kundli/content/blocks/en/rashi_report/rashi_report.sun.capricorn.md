Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, confidence is steady and practical, built through reliable effort and visible results. The sign lord Saturn is not friendly to Sun, so results may come with some effort.
