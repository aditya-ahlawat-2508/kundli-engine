Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, affection is friendly and refined, enjoying conversation, art and social grace. The sign lord Saturn is friendly to Venus, so the placement is generally supportive.
