Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, wisdom is compassionate and devotional, expanding through care, faith and imagination. The sign lord Mars is friendly to Jupiter, so the placement is generally supportive.
