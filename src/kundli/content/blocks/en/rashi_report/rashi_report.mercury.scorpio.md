Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, thinking is intuitive and imaginative, absorbing impressions more than arguments. The sign lord Mars is neutral to Mercury, giving moderate and mixed support.
