Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, ambition is drawn to technology, media, networks and new ideas. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
