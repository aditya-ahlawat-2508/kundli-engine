Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, energy is emotional and protective, strong when motivated by feeling and prone to moods. Mars is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
