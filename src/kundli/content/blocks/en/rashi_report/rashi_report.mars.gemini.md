Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, energy goes into talk, planning and mental contests, sharp but sometimes scattered. The sign lord Mercury is not friendly to Mars, so results may come with some effort.
