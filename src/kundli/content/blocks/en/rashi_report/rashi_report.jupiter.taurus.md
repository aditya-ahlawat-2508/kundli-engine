Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, wisdom is practical and prudent, growing through steady, ethical building. The sign lord Venus is not friendly to Jupiter, so results may come with some effort.
