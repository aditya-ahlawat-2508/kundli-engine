Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, discipline is natural and enduring, giving patience, thrift and capacity for hard work. The sign lord Mercury is friendly to Saturn, so the placement is generally supportive.
