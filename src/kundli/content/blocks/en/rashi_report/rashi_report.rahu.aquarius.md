Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, ambition is drawn to technology, media, networks and new ideas. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
