Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, confidence is quiet and feeling-led, drawing strength from intuition and loyalty. The sign lord Jupiter is friendly to Sun, so the placement is generally supportive.
