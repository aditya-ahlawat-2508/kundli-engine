Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, affection is loyal and sensual, valuing comfort, stability and beautiful things. The sign lord Saturn is friendly to Venus, so the placement is generally supportive.
