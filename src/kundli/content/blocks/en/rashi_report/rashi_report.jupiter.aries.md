Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, wisdom is inspired and optimistic, favouring vision, faith and bold growth. The sign lord Mars is friendly to Jupiter, so the placement is generally supportive.
