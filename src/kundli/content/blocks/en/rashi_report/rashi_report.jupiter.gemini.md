Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, wisdom is intellectual and sociable, expanding through learning, teaching and networks. The sign lord Mercury is not friendly to Jupiter, so results may come with some effort.
