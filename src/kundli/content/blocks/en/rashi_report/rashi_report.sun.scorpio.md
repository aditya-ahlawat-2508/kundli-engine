Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, confidence is quiet and feeling-led, drawing strength from intuition and loyalty. The sign lord Mars is friendly to Sun, so the placement is generally supportive.
