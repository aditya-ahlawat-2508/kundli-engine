Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, detachment shows as restless independence and a wish to act without applause. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
