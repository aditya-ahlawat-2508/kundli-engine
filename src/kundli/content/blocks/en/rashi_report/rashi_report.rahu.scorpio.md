Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, ambition mixes with emotion and imagination, drawn to the hidden, the foreign or the mystical. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
