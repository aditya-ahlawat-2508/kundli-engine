Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, emotions are steady and grounded, seeking security, routine and tangible comfort. The sign lord Mercury is friendly to Moon, so the placement is generally supportive.
