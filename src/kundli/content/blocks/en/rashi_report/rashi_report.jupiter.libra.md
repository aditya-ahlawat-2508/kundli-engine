Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, wisdom is intellectual and sociable, expanding through learning, teaching and networks. The sign lord Venus is not friendly to Jupiter, so results may come with some effort.
