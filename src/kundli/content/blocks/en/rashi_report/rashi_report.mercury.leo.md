Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, thinking is fast, decisive and persuasive, sometimes impatient with detail. The sign lord Sun is friendly to Mercury, so the placement is generally supportive.
