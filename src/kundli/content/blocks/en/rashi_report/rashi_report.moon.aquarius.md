Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, emotions are processed through thought and conversation, with a curious, restless mind. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
