Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, confidence works through ideas, communication and the company of others. Sun is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
