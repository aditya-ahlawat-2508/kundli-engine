Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, detachment shows as deep intuition and a pull toward inner, spiritual experience. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
