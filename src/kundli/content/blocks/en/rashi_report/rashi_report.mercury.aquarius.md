Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, thinking is versatile and social, quick to learn, connect and exchange ideas. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Mercury, giving moderate and mixed support.
