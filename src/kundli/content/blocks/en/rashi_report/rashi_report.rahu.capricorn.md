Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, ambition focuses on money, status and tangible achievement, often by unconventional means. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
