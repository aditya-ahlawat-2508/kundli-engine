Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, energy is direct and competitive, quick to start things and to defend its position. The sign lord Jupiter is friendly to Mars, so the placement is generally supportive.
