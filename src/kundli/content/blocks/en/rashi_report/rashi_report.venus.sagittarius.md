Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, affection is passionate and expressive, drawn to excitement and admiration. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Venus, giving moderate and mixed support.
