Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, detachment shows as simplicity and indifference to material display. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
