Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, thinking is methodical and practical, strong in analysis, accounts and craft. Mercury is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
