Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, affection is loyal and sensual, valuing comfort, stability and beautiful things. Venus is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
