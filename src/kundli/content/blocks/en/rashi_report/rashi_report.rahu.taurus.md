Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, ambition focuses on money, status and tangible achievement, often by unconventional means. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
