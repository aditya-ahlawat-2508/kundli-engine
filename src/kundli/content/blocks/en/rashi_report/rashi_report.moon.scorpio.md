Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, emotions run deep and receptive, with strong empathy, memory and sensitivity to atmosphere. Moon is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
