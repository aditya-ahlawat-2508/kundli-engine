Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, discipline is tested by impatience and learns to channel drive into long-term goals. Saturn is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
