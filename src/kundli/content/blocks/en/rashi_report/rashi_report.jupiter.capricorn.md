Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, wisdom is practical and prudent, growing through steady, ethical building. Jupiter is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
