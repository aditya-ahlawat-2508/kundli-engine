Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, energy goes into talk, planning and mental contests, sharp but sometimes scattered. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Mars, giving moderate and mixed support.
