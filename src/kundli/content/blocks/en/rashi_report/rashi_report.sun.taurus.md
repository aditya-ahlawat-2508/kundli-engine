Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, confidence is steady and practical, built through reliable effort and visible results. The sign lord Venus is not friendly to Sun, so results may come with some effort.
