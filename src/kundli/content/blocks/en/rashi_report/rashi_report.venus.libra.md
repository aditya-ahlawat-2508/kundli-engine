Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, affection is friendly and refined, enjoying conversation, art and social grace. Venus is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
