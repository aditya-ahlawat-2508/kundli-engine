Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, wisdom is intellectual and sociable, expanding through learning, teaching and networks. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Jupiter, giving moderate and mixed support.
