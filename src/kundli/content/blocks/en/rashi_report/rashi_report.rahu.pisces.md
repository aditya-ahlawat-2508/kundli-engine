Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, ambition mixes with emotion and imagination, drawn to the hidden, the foreign or the mystical. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
