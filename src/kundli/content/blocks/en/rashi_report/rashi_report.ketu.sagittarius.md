Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, detachment shows as restless independence and a wish to act without applause. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
