Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, ambition is bold and attention-seeking, drawn to leadership and risk. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
