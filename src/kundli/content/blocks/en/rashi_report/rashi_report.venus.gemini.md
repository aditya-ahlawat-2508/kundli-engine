Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, affection is friendly and refined, enjoying conversation, art and social grace. The sign lord Mercury is friendly to Venus, so the placement is generally supportive.
