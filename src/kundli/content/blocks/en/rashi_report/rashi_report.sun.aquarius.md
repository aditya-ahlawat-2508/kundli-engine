Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, confidence works through ideas, communication and the company of others. The sign lord Saturn is not friendly to Sun, so results may come with some effort.
