Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, discipline works through systems, rules and ideas, with serious, detached reasoning. The sign lord Mercury is friendly to Saturn, so the placement is generally supportive.
