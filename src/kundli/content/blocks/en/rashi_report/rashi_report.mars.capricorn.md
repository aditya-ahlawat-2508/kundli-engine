Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, energy is patient and sustained, best used on concrete projects that need stamina. Mars is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
