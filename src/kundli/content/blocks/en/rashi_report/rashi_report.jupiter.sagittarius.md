Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, wisdom is inspired and optimistic, favouring vision, faith and bold growth. Jupiter is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
