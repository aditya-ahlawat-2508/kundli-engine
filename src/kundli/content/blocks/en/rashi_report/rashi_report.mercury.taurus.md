Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, thinking is methodical and practical, strong in analysis, accounts and craft. The sign lord Venus is friendly to Mercury, so the placement is generally supportive.
