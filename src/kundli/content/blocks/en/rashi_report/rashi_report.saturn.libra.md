Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, discipline works through systems, rules and ideas, with serious, detached reasoning. Saturn is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
