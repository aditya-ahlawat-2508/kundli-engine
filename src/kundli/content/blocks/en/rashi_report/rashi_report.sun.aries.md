Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, confidence is open and bold, with a natural urge to lead and be seen. Sun is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
