Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, thinking is methodical and practical, strong in analysis, accounts and craft. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Mercury, giving moderate and mixed support.
