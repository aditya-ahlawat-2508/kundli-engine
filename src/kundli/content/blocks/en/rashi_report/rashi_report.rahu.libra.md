Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, ambition is drawn to technology, media, networks and new ideas. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
