Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, discipline is tested by impatience and learns to channel drive into long-term goals. The sign lord Sun is not friendly to Saturn, so results may come with some effort.
