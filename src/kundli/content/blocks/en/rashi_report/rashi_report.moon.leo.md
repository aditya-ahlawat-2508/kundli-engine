Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, emotions are quick and warm, reacting with enthusiasm and a need for action. The sign lord Sun is friendly to Moon, so the placement is generally supportive.
