Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, wisdom is compassionate and devotional, expanding through care, faith and imagination. Jupiter is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
