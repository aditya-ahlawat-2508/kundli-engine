Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, thinking is intuitive and imaginative, absorbing impressions more than arguments. Mercury is debilitated here, so these qualities may need conscious effort and tend to mature with experience.
