Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Virgo, a dual earth sign ruled by Mercury and marked by precision, service and practical improvement, wisdom is practical and prudent, growing through steady, ethical building. The sign lord Mercury is not friendly to Jupiter, so results may come with some effort.
