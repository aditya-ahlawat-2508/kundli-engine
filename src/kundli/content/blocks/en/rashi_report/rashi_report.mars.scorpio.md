Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, energy is emotional and protective, strong when motivated by feeling and prone to moods. Mars is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
