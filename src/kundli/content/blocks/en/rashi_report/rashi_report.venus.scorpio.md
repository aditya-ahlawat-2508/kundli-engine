Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Scorpio, a fixed water sign ruled by Mars and marked by intensity, depth and determination to transform, affection is tender and devoted, emotionally deep and romantic. The sign lord Mars is neutral to Venus, giving moderate and mixed support.
