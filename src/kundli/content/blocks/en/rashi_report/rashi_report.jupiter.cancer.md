Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, wisdom is compassionate and devotional, expanding through care, faith and imagination. Jupiter is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
