Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, energy is direct and competitive, quick to start things and to defend its position. Mars is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
