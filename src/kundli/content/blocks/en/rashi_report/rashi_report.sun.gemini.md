Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Gemini, a dual air sign ruled by Mercury and marked by curiosity, versatility and quick communication, confidence works through ideas, communication and the company of others. The sign lord Mercury is neutral to Sun, giving moderate and mixed support.
