Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, energy goes into talk, planning and mental contests, sharp but sometimes scattered. The sign lord Venus is neutral to Mars, giving moderate and mixed support.
