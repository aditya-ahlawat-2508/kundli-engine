Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, detachment shows as simplicity and indifference to material display. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
