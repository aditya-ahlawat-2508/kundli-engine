Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, emotions run deep and receptive, with strong empathy, memory and sensitivity to atmosphere. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
