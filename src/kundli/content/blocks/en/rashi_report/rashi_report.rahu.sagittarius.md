Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, ambition is bold and attention-seeking, drawn to leadership and risk. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
