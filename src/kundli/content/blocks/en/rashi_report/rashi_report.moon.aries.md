Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, emotions are quick and warm, reacting with enthusiasm and a need for action. The sign lord Mars is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
