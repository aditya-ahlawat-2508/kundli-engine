Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, thinking is intuitive and imaginative, absorbing impressions more than arguments. The sign lord Moon is not friendly to Mercury, so results may come with some effort.
