Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, affection is passionate and expressive, drawn to excitement and admiration. The sign lord Sun is not friendly to Venus, so results may come with some effort.
