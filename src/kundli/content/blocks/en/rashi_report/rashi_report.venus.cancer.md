Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, affection is tender and devoted, emotionally deep and romantic. The sign lord Moon is not friendly to Venus, so results may come with some effort.
