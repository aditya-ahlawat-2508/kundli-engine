Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, detachment shows as simplicity and indifference to material display. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
