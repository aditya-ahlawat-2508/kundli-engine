Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In Aries, a movable fire sign ruled by Mars and marked by initiative, courage and a pioneering spirit, ambition is bold and attention-seeking, drawn to leadership and risk. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
