Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Pisces, a dual water sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by compassion, imagination and spiritual sensitivity, discipline meets emotion, with caution in feelings and a need to build trust slowly. The sign lord Jupiter is neutral to Saturn, giving moderate and mixed support.
