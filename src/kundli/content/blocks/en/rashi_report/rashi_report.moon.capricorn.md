Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Capricorn, a movable earth sign ruled by Saturn and marked by ambition, structure and responsibility, emotions are steady and grounded, seeking security, routine and tangible comfort. The sign lord Saturn is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
