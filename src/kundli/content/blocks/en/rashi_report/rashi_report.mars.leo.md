Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, energy is direct and competitive, quick to start things and to defend its position. The sign lord Sun is friendly to Mars, so the placement is generally supportive.
