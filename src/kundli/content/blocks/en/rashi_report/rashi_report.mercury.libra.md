Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, thinking is versatile and social, quick to learn, connect and exchange ideas. The sign lord Venus is friendly to Mercury, so the placement is generally supportive.
