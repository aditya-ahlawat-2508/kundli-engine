Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, confidence is quiet and feeling-led, drawing strength from intuition and loyalty. The sign lord Moon is friendly to Sun, so the placement is generally supportive.
