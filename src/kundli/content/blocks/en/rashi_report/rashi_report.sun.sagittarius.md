Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Sagittarius, a dual fire sign ruled by Jupiter and marked by optimism, philosophy and a love of freedom and exploration, confidence is open and bold, with a natural urge to lead and be seen. The sign lord Jupiter is friendly to Sun, so the placement is generally supportive.
