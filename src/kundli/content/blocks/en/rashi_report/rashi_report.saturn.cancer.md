Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In Cancer, a movable water sign ruled by Moon and marked by nurturing, protectiveness and emotional memory, discipline meets emotion, with caution in feelings and a need to build trust slowly. The sign lord Moon is not friendly to Saturn, so results may come with some effort.
