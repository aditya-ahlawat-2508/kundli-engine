Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, affection is loyal and sensual, valuing comfort, stability and beautiful things. Venus is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
