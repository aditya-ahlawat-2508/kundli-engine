Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Taurus, a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus and marked by patience, persistence and a love of stability and comfort, emotions are steady and grounded, seeking security, routine and tangible comfort. Moon is exalted here, so these qualities are traditionally considered strongly supported.
