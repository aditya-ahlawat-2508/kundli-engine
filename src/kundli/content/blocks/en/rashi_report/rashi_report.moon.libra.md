Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, emotions are processed through thought and conversation, with a curious, restless mind. The sign lord Venus is neutral to Moon, giving moderate and mixed support.
