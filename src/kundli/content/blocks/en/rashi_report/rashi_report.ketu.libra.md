Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Libra, a movable air sign ruled by Venus and marked by balance, diplomacy and a sense of fairness and beauty, detachment shows as a questioning, abstract mind that steps back from social noise. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
