Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, detachment shows as restless independence and a wish to act without applause. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
