Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, confidence is open and bold, with a natural urge to lead and be seen. Sun is in its own sign here, a comfortable and self-supported placement.
