Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In Leo, a fixed fire sign ruled by Sun and marked by dignity, generosity and a need for recognition, wisdom is inspired and optimistic, favouring vision, faith and bold growth. The sign lord Sun is friendly to Jupiter, so the placement is generally supportive.
