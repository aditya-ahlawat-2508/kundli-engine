Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In Aquarius, a fixed air sign ruled by Saturn and marked by independence, originality and concern for the wider group, detachment shows as a questioning, abstract mind that steps back from social noise. For the nodes this report follows the convention stated in its appendix.
