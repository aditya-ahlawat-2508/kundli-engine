Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In the 12th house, which concerns expenses, foreign lands and solitude, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Moon supports emotional steadiness and adaptability; a weaker Moon asks for regular routines and restful habits.
