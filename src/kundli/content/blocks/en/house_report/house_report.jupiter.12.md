Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In the 12th house, which concerns expenses, foreign lands and solitude, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Jupiter supports wisdom, guidance and good fortune; a weaker Jupiter asks you to stay open to learning and counsel.
