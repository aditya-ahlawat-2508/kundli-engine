Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In the 9th house, which concerns fortune, dharma and higher learning, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Mars supports courage and initiative; a weaker Mars asks you to channel energy patiently and avoid haste.
