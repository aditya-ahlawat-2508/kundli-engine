Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In the 4th house, which concerns home, mother and inner peace, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Jupiter supports wisdom, guidance and good fortune; a weaker Jupiter asks you to stay open to learning and counsel.
