Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In the 3rd house, which concerns effort, courage and siblings, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Mercury supports clear thinking and communication; a weaker Mercury asks for focus and careful checking of details.
