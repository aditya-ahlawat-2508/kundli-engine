Venus signifies love, harmony, beauty, comfort, the arts, partnership and pleasure. In the 3rd house, which concerns effort, courage and siblings, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Venus supports harmony in relationships and enjoyment of life; a weaker Venus asks for balance between comfort and commitment.
