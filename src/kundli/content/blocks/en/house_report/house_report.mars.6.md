Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In the 6th house, which concerns daily work, service and obstacles, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Mars supports courage and initiative; a weaker Mars asks you to channel energy patiently and avoid haste.
