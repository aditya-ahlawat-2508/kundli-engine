Mercury signifies intellect, speech, learning, trade, analysis and adaptability. In the 9th house, which concerns fortune, dharma and higher learning, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Mercury supports clear thinking and communication; a weaker Mercury asks for focus and careful checking of details.
