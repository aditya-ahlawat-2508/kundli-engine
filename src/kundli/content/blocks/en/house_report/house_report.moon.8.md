Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In the 8th house, which concerns transformation and sudden change, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Moon supports emotional steadiness and adaptability; a weaker Moon asks for regular routines and restful habits.
