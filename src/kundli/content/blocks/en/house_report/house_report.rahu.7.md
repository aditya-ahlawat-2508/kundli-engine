Rahu signifies ambition, innovation, foreign influences, unconventional paths and worldly desire. In the 7th house, which concerns marriage and partnerships, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. Rahu amplifies whatever it touches and tends to bring sudden changes of direction; grounding and ethics keep its ambition constructive.
