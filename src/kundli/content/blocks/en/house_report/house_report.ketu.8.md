Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In the 8th house, which concerns transformation and sudden change, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. Ketu points to areas where you feel naturally detached; mindful engagement keeps its insight from turning into withdrawal.
