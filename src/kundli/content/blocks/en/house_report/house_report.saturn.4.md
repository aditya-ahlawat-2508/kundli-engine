Saturn signifies discipline, duty, time, perseverance, structure and long-term results. In the 4th house, which concerns home, mother and inner peace, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Saturn supports discipline and lasting achievement; a weaker Saturn asks for patience, since results tend to arrive late but stay.
