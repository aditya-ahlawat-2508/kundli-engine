Jupiter signifies wisdom, growth, teachers, fortune, ethics, children and generosity. In the 1st house, which concerns self and personality, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Jupiter supports wisdom, guidance and good fortune; a weaker Jupiter asks you to stay open to learning and counsel.
