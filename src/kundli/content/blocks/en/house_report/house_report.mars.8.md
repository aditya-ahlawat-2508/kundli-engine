Mars signifies energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings and physical effort. In the 8th house, which concerns transformation and sudden change, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Mars supports courage and initiative; a weaker Mars asks you to channel energy patiently and avoid haste.
