Ketu signifies detachment, intuition, spirituality, past-life themes and letting go. In the 4th house, which concerns home, mother and inner peace, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. Ketu points to areas where you feel naturally detached; mindful engagement keeps its insight from turning into withdrawal.
