Sun signifies the soul, vitality, authority, father figures, confidence and leadership. In the 7th house, which concerns marriage and partnerships, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Sun supports self-belief and a clear sense of purpose; a weaker Sun asks you to build confidence through service and steady effort.
