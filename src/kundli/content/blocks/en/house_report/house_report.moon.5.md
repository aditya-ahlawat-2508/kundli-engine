Moon signifies the mind, emotions, mother, nurturing, memory and the public mood. In the 5th house, which concerns intellect, creativity and children, these themes tend to show up through that area of life. A well-placed Moon supports emotional steadiness and adaptability; a weaker Moon asks for regular routines and restful habits.
