Pearl is the traditional gem of the Moon, associated with calm, emotional balance and receptivity. In Jyotish a gemstone is a symbolic support, not a medical, legal or financial remedy, and results are not guaranteed. Strong stones (Blue Sapphire, Hessonite, Cat's Eye) are traditionally tried for a short period before regular wear. Consult a qualified astrologer and buy only from a certified gemologist.
