At the reference date of this report Sade Sati is active (Dhaiya (Saturn 4th from the Moon)). Traditionally advised: steady routines, honesty, patience and respect for elders and colleagues. Traditional practices include reciting the Hanuman Chalisa, serving elders and the needy, and charity on Saturdays. They are devotional and cultural; no outcome is guaranteed.
